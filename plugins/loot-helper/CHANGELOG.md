# Loot Helper changelog

## 0.2.0 - 2026-10-15

### Changed
- Settings moved to maoloader's shared settings window: Ctrl+K → **Plugin settings** → Loot Helper. Your saved settings carry over. Changing settings needs maoloader 0.1.7 or newer.
- The disenchant preview and button now live in its settings tab. On maoloader 0.1.6 or older, update maoloader to use them.

## 0.1.0 - 2026-10-15

First release. Preview and bulk-disenchant champion shards for Blue Essence, with a confirm step.
