# Friend Alerts changelog

## 0.2.1 - 2026-10-18

### Changed
- Works with Streamer Mode: while it's on, toasts say "A friend" instead of a name and leave out status messages.

## 0.2.0 - 2026-10-15

### Changed
- Settings moved to maoloader's shared settings window: Ctrl+K → **Plugin settings** → Friend Alerts. Your saved settings carry over. Changing settings needs maoloader 0.1.7 or newer.

## 0.1.0 - 2026-10-15

First release. Toasts when chosen friends come online or finish a game, with a quick invite while you're in a lobby.
