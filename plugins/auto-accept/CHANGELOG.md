# Auto-accept changelog

## 0.2.0 - 2026-10-15

### Changed
- Settings moved to maoloader's shared settings window: Ctrl+K → **Plugin settings** → Auto Accept. Your saved settings carry over. Changing settings needs maoloader 0.1.7 or newer.

## 0.1.0 - 2026-10-15

First release. Accept ready checks automatically after a short delay you can cancel from a toast.
