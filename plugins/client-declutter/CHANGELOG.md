# Declutter changelog

## 0.2.0 - 2026-10-15

### Changed
- Settings moved to maoloader's shared settings window: Ctrl+K → **Plugin settings** → Declutter. Your saved settings carry over. Changing settings needs maoloader 0.1.7 or newer.
- **Show everything** is now a switch at the top of the settings.

## 0.1.0 - 2026-10-15

First release. Hide League client clutter with toggleable presets and your own CSS selectors.
