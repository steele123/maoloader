# Rune Presets changelog

## 0.2.0 - 2026-10-15

### Changed
- Settings moved to maoloader's shared settings window: Ctrl+K → **Plugin settings** → Rune Presets. Your saved settings carry over. Changing settings needs maoloader 0.1.7 or newer.

## 0.1.0 - 2026-10-15

First release. Save rune pages as named presets, apply them in one click, and auto-apply them per champion.
